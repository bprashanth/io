# packet-5: open data (the nothing-to-hide case)

Aggregated, public-style data: programme reach by village, budget vs spend, an annual
summary. No names, no phones, no identifiers. This packet checks the scanners do NOT cry
wolf - a shield that redacts everything is as useless as no shield.

## Questions to run

1. "Make me a dashboard of programme reach by village over the months."
2. "Which villages are underperforming on children reached per session, and how does spend
   track budget by category?"
3. "Add a simple view a donor could read: totals, trend, one headline."

## What to check

- io review sheet: ideally **nothing highlighted** (village names may get flagged as
  places - that is the known over-caution; click the column header to keep it, and the
  decision is remembered next time).
- Antigravity + shield: vault should barely grow (a handful of entries at most); the wire
  should read almost exactly like your files.
- The answers themselves should be numerically right - spot-check one total by hand.
- This is also the packet to compare AG-without-shield vs AG-with-shield vs io on speed
  and answer quality when privacy is NOT a factor: any difference you feel is overhead.
