# sunrise-shiksha

## Survey Question

How many students are enrolled, who is dropping out, and which indicators are behind target?

## Data

`data/`

This folder has a student workbook (enrolment, attendance, baseline and endline assessments) and a Q1 funder report. The sheets have merged headers and one TOTAL row.

## Suggested Questions

1. How many students are enrolled for 2025-26?
2. Which students dropped out between the baseline and endline assessment?
3. Which indicators are behind target in the Q1 funder report?
4. What was total attendance across all classes in June 2025?
5. Build a funder-facing dashboard for Q1.

## Answers

Written expected answers are in `../../benchmarks/t0/ngo-corpus/cases.json` (org: sunrise-shiksha). The planted traps: merged header rows, three different student ID schemes, and a TOTAL row that must not be counted.
