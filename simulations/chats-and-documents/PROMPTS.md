## Survey Question

How can we understand what is getting lost in field WhatsApp groups and documents?

## Data

`../../benchmarks/t0/text-fixtures/`

This folder has chat exports, field notes, a helpline log, a report extract, and a PDF letter.

Optional extra data:

`../../benchmarks/t0/ngo-corpus/gram-sudhar/`

## Suggested Questions

1. Summarise the main issues from the field chat.
2. What do people report regularly, and in what format?
3. Make a weekly summary table from the chat.
4. How many meetings are discussed in the field chat?
5. Which issues need follow-up from the team?
