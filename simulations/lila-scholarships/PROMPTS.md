# lila-scholarships

## Survey Question

Can we compare scholarship applicant lists and find missing or duplicate students?

## Data

`data/`

This folder has scholarship applicant lists for two years and disbursement data.

## Suggested Questions

1. Make a dashboard for this year's scholarship applicants and payments.
2. Find likely duplicate applicants across both years.
3. Show who is fully paid, partly paid, or not yet paid.
4. Show total disbursement by instalment.
