Annual Programme Summary 2025-26 (public version)

Across twelve villages in Pune district the programme held 1,842 sessions reaching a
cumulative 38,400 child-attendances. Volunteer strength grew from 61 to 104. Three new
community learning centres opened. Budget utilisation stood at 87 percent, with Transport
and Materials the largest heads. The dropout rate fell from 14 to 9 percent over the year.
All figures aggregated; no individual records appear in this document.
