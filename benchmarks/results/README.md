# Results

Aggregate results are derived from immutable raw records under `../runs/`.
Every table names the benchmark version, case count, repetitions, systems,
model identifiers, exclusions, critical failures, uncertainty method, and the
script/commit that produced it.

Do not hand-edit an aggregate to correct a run. Correct or invalidate the raw
record, document why in chronology, and regenerate the aggregate.

Current files deliberately represent different claim scopes:

- `v2-local-first-ladder-2026-08-21.json` is the current event-prototype
  decision. It links the frozen local-model gates, routed replay, rendered
  cross-shape journeys, privacy scan and later Antigravity agriculture runs. It
  explicitly excludes Windows, local-Qwen and concurrency claims;
- `screening-v2-counted.json` is the primary counted product screen;
- `screening-qwen35-9b-default-v1-futility.json` is an early-stop model screen;
- `deepseek-web-qwen38-27b-guardrail-v2-development.json` is named alternative-
  harness development evidence and cannot establish Cline or Antigravity GUI
  equivalence;
- `product-gui-smoke-v1-diagnostic.json` is the completed one-case real-editor
  pairing of Antigravity IDE and two Cline/Qwen reasoning-effort variants. It
  supports benchmark reformulation and finalist selection, not a statistical
  equivalence claim.

The product-GUI raw runs remain under `../runs/2026-08-20-product-gui-smoke/`.
The diagnostic aggregate now exists because the paired Antigravity IDE artifact
is complete. It remains uncounted: both Cline variants reused an existing
client profile, Antigravity needed a default-model initialisation workaround,
and each system has only one unblinded repetition.
