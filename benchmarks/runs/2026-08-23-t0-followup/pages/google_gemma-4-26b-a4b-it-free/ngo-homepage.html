Empowering Communities

What We Do

Jagruti Foundation works at the intersection of education, health, and livelihood. We believe that true empowerment comes from providing communities with the tools to solve their own challenges. By working directly with local leaders, we ensure our interventions are culturally relevant and long-lasting.

Our Programmes

Education for All

Bridging the literacy gap by providing resources and support to underprivileged schools and learning centers.

Health & Wellness

Improving community health through awareness campaigns, nutrition programs, and local clinic support.

Livelihood Support

Empowering individuals through vocational training and micro-entrepreneurship opportunities.
