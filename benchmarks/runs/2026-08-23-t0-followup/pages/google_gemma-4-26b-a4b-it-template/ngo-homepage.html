Jagruti Foundation

Empowering Communities for a Brighter Tomorrow

We work at the grassroots level to create sustainable impact through education, health, and livelihood empowerment.

What We Do

Jagruti Foundation is dedicated to breaking the cycle of poverty by providing essential resources and opportunities to underserved populations. We believe in community-led development that ensures long-term resilience.

Our Programmes

Education for All

Providing scholarships and learning resources to children in rural areas.

Health & Wellness

Mobile clinics and maternal health awareness programs for remote villages.

Skill Development

Vocational training for women to foster financial independence.
