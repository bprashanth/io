Awakening Communities, Enabling Self-Reliance

Jagruti Foundation works alongside marginalized rural and urban communities to build sustainable livelihoods, improve health, and ensure quality education for all children.

What We Do

We believe in grassroots empowerment. We do not just bring aid; we build long-term local capacity so communities can lead their own growth.

Community Mobilisation

We work directly with village development committees and women's self-help groups to identify core problems and build local action plans.

Skill & Capacity Building

Through practical workshops and ongoing mentoring, we help youth and adults acquire sustainable, income-generating skills.

Our Programmes

Focused interventions designed to create long-lasting, measurable social impact.

Education

Shiksha Setu

Bridging foundational learning gaps for children in primary grades. We operate after-school learning centres and equip local schools with practical learning materials.

Livelihoods

Gram Samriddhi

Empowering smallholder farmers and women artisans with climate-smart agricultural techniques, market linkages, and financial literacy training.

Healthcare

Aarogya Sangam

Delivering primary preventive healthcare, maternal and child nutrition awareness, and routine diagnostic camps in remote rural settlements.
