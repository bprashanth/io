Jagruti Foundation

Awakening Potential, Transforming Lives

Jagruti Foundation is dedicated to driving grassroots change through education, sustainable livelihoods, and community healthcare initiatives across underserved regions.

What We Do

Founded on the principles of equity and community participation, Jagruti Foundation partners directly with rural and semi-urban communities to co-create sustainable solutions. We bridge gaps in foundational learning, nurture micro-entrepreneurs, and promote preventive healthcare to ensure that every individual has an opportunity to thrive with dignity.

Our Three Programmes

1. Vidya Jyoti

Foundational literacy, digital learning centres, and scholarship support for first-generation school goers in rural areas.

2. Swavalamban

Vocational skill training, self-help group micro-grants, and market linkage programs for women and youth.

3. Aarogya Mitra

Mobile health camps, maternal & child nutrition awareness, and clean drinking water community interventions.
