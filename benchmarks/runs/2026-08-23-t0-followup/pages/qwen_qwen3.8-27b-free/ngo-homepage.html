What we do

Small, steady work that lasts

We don't parachute in with big promises. We build three things that make a community stronger from the inside out.

📚

Learning for life

Practical skills and basic education that people can use every day — from reading and maths to running a small business.

🤝

Local leadership

We find and train volunteers from the neighbourhood so decisions are made by the people who live there.

🌱

Health & well-being

Simple, affordable health awareness and clean-water habits that keep families well and reduce the need for hospitals.

Our three programmes

Where we put our effort

Three focused programmes, each designed to hand the keys back to the community.

Programme 01

Shiksha — Learning Circles

Small evening classes in homes and community halls where adults and children learn together. We provide books, a teacher's guide, and a patient volunteer to keep the circle going.

Who it helps: families in low-income neighbourhoods
Programme 02

Swasthya — Health Champions

We train one or two residents per street as health champions. They run clean-water checks, vaccination reminders, and simple first-aid sessions right in their own lane.

Who it helps: whole streets and small towns
Programme 03

Kaushal — Skills & Savings

Short, hands-on courses in a trade or craft, paired with a small group savings circle. People earn a skill and build a little money together, one week at a time.

Who it helps: young adults looking for work
