This dashboard provides an overview of the handpump survey data, focusing on functionality, water quality, and repair status across different blocks. The first section highlights the total number of handpumps and the count of those currently functional, giving a quick snapshot of the infrastructure's operational status.

The second section delves into water quality metrics, specifically the Total Dissolved Solids (TDS) levels. It presents the average and maximum TDS values for each block, allowing for a comparison of water safety standards across the surveyed areas. This data is crucial for assessing the health implications of the water sources.

Finally, the dashboard examines the repair status of the handpumps. By categorizing them as 'Safe', 'Unsafe', or 'Not Tested', it offers insights into the maintenance needs of the infrastructure. The recent repairs table lists the most recently serviced handpumps, providing a timeline of maintenance activities and their locations.
