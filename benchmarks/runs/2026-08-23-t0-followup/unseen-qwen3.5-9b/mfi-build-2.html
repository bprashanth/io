SHG Repayment Performance

Overview of repayment totals, monthly trends, and block-wise performance

The dashboard highlights the total volume of repayments collected across all Self Help Groups (SHGs) and identifies the number of members currently active in the program. It also tracks the count of SHGs facing overdue status to monitor risk exposure.

The bar chart breaks down the total repaid amount by geographic block, allowing the bank to see which regions are performing best. The line chart visualizes the aggregate repayment flow month-over-month, smoothing out individual SHG variations to show the overall health of the portfolio.

Finally, the table lists the top-performing SHGs by total repayment amount, providing specific examples of successful groups. This data helps in recognizing high-performing blocks and understanding the distribution of repayment success.

Monthly Repayment Trend

How this was computed · 12 rows
WITH unpivoted AS (SELECT "SHG Name", "Block", "Members", "Loan Amount", "Total Repaid", "Overdue (Y/N)", UNNEST(ARRAY['Apr-25', 'May-25', 'Jun-25', 'Jul-25', 'Aug-25', 'Sep-25', 'Oct-25', 'Nov-25', 'Dec-25', 'Jan-26', 'Feb-26', 'Mar-26']) AS month, UNNEST(ARRAY["Apr-25", "May-25", "Jun-25", "Jul-25", "Aug-25", "Sep-25", "Oct-25", "Nov-25", "Dec-25", "Jan-26", "Feb-26", "Mar-26"]) AS amount FROM "shg_repayment_ledger") SELECT month, SUM(amount) AS total FROM unpivoted WHERE amount IS NOT NULL GROUP BY 1 ORDER BY 1
