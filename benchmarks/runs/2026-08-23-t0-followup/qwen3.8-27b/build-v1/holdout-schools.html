The dashboard covers 40 schools with a total 2024 enrolment of 9,343 students.

Enrolment trends show a steady increase from 2022 to 2024 for both genders, with girls' enrolment reaching 4,893 and boys' enrolment reaching 4,450 in 2024.

In 2024, girls account for 4,893 students while boys account for 4,450 students.

How this was computed · 2 rows

Infrastructure Gaps

How this was computed · 3 rows
SELECT 'No Girls Toilets' AS gap, COUNT(*) AS count FROM "school_infrastructure" WHERE "Toilets functional (girls)" = 'No' UNION ALL SELECT 'No Drinking Water', COUNT(*) FROM "school_infrastructure" WHERE "Drinking water" = 'No' UNION ALL SELECT 'No Electricity', COUNT(*) FROM "school_infrastructure" WHERE "Electricity" = 'No'
