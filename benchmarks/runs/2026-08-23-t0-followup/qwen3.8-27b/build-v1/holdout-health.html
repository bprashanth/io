This dashboard tracks 20 districts across three states. The average rate of institutional births is 74.66, while the average stunting rate among children under five is 37.91.

The district with the highest institutional birth rate is Purnia (89.6), indicating stronger access to facility-based care. Conversely, the district with the highest stunting rate is Kishanganj (48.15), highlighting a critical area for nutritional intervention.

Vaccination coverage varies significantly, with Rayagada (73.65) leading in full immunization for children aged 12-23 months. The table below provides a detailed breakdown of these key indicators for all tracked districts.

Institutional Births by District

How this was computed · 20 rows
