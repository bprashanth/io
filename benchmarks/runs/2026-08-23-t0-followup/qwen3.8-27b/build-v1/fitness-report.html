Fitness Programme Impact Summary

Comparing baseline and endline physical metrics for enrolled children

The fitness programme engaged 200 children across various locations.

Overall, participants showed an average improvement in shuttle run time of 0.0135 seconds, indicating better speed and agility.

Strength metrics also improved, with an average increase of 2.92 sit-ups per child.

The top performing location in terms of fastest shuttle run times is Barachatti (10.44), while the gender with the highest average sit-up count is Male (20.48).
