SHG Loan Portfolio & Repayment Health

Analysis of loan distribution by purpose and repayment status across blocks

The SHG loan portfolio comprises 360 loans with a total disbursed amount of 1.78 Cr. This indicates a significant financial commitment to community development across the surveyed blocks.

Repayment health is a critical metric for sustainability. Currently, 60 loans are marked as overdue. The distribution of repayment statuses is visualized in the pie chart, where Regular (156) represents the largest segment, indicating the dominant status of the portfolio.

Loan purposes vary, with the bar chart showing the financial weight of each category. The top purpose by loan amount is Agriculture (53.35 L), suggesting a primary focus on this sector. Other purposes follow in descending order of financial volume.

Geographically, the top blocks by loan volume are listed in the table. The leading block is Fatehpur (33.05 L), which accounts for a significant portion of the total disbursements. This concentration may require targeted monitoring to ensure repayment compliance.
