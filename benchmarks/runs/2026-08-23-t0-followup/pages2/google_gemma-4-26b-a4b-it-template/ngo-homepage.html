What We Do

Jagruti Foundation works at the intersection of education, health, and livelihood. We believe that sustainable change begins with empowering individuals to take charge of their own development through structured support and resource accessibility.

Our Programmes

Education for All

Providing learning materials and tutoring to underprivileged children to bridge the literacy gap.

Health & Hygiene

Community workshops and sanitation drives to ensure basic wellness for rural families.

Skill Development

Vocational training for youth to foster financial independence and employment.
