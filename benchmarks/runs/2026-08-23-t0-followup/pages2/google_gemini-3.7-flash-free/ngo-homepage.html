Empowering Communities, Enabling Change

Jagruti Foundation works alongside grassroots communities to build sustainable pathways for education, health, and livelihood.

Get in Touch

What We Do

We partner directly with local families, community leaders, and local institutions to address systemic challenges through grassroots action.

Community Action

Facilitating local self-help groups and youth networks to identify needs and lead development projects in their own villages.

Capacity Building

Providing training, skill-building workshops, and technical tools to ensure long-term independence and resilience.

Sustainable Support

Designing interventions with local resources that continue to benefit communities long after initial projects conclude.

Our Three Programmes

Focused initiatives designed to address core community needs comprehensively.

Programme 01

Shiksha: Foundational Learning

Supporting rural primary schools with remedial learning centers, teacher aid programs, and library setups to ensure every child reads at grade level.

Programme 02

Aarogya: Community Health

Organizing mobile health clinics, maternal nutrition drives, and clean water awareness campaigns for underserved rural settlements.

Programme 03

Hunarmand: Women's Livelihoods

Providing vocational skill training, financial literacy coaching, and market linkage support to enable women to run micro-enterprises.
