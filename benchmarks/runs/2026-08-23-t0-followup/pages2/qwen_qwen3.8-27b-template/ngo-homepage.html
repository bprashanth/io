Jagruti Foundation

What we do

Jagruti Foundation works with communities to build lasting, self-reliant change. We focus on the foundations of a good life — learning, health, and the ability to earn a fair living — and we stay in a place long enough to see the change take root.

Our name, Jagruti, means awakening. We believe every community already holds the answers; our role is to help them find and use them.
