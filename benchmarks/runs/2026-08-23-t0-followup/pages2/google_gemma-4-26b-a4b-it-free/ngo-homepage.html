What We Do

Jagruti Foundation works at the intersection of education, health, and livelihood. We believe that true empowerment comes from providing communities with the tools they need to build their own future. Our approach is hands-on, local, and long-term.

Our Programmes

Education for All

Providing resources, tutoring, and digital literacy to children in underserved areas.

Health & Wellness

Mobile clinics and hygiene awareness programs to ensure community well-being.

Livelihood Support

Skill development and micro-entrepreneurship training for women and youth.
