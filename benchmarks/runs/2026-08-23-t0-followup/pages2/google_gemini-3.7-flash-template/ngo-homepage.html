Jagruti Foundation

Empowering Communities, Transforming Lives

Jagruti Foundation is dedicated to fostering grassroots development, holistic education, healthcare access, and sustainable livelihood opportunities across underprivileged regions.

What We Do

We partner directly with rural and peri-urban communities to identify structural challenges and implement long-term, community-owned solutions. Through continuous capacity building, resource deployment, and on-ground mentorship, we build resilient ecosystems that enable families to break systemic cycles of poverty.
