Jagruti Foundation

What We Do

Jagruti Foundation is dedicated to empowering communities through education, sustainable development, and social welfare. We believe in creating a future where every individual has the opportunity to thrive.

Our Programmes

Education for All

Providing quality education to underprivileged children through scholarships, school infrastructure, and teacher training.

Health & Wellness

Organizing health camps, awareness drives, and providing medical aid to rural communities.

Sustainable Livelihood

Empowering women and youth with vocational skills and micro-finance to build self-sustaining economies.
