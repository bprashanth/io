Olive ridley hatchery, Rushikulya, volunteer rota for March

Night patrol 10 pm to 4 am: Meena, Arjun (Mon, Thu); Farhan, Lata (Tue, Fri); Sunil alone on Sundays.
Move any nest laid below the high tide line into the hatchery within 2 hours. Note the nest number and egg count.
Olive ridleys are a threatened species; do not use torches on the beach, use the red lamps only.
Hatchlings are released at dawn only. Keep dogs off the beach.
