Set up Idli

Two steps, once. Then Idli opens.

1 Connect to Idli's model

Paste the code you were sent. It starts with idli1.

2 Connect Claude (optional)

Claude teaches Idli new kinds of files, using made-up copies only. Without it, Idli still finds, checks and answers questions about files it already knows.

3 Google

When Idli opens, sign in to Google in its Drive tab, then say yes to indexing your Drive.
