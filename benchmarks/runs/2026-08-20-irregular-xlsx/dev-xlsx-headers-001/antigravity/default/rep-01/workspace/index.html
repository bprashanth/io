              📖 Data Dictionary & Indicator Definitions
            

          

          

            
    
              
  • Boys Attendance (%): Average percentage of enrolled male primary school students present during the academic year.
    • 
              
  • Girls Attendance (%): Average percentage of enrolled female primary school students present during the academic year.
    • 
              
  • Average Attendance (%): Arithmetic mean of Boys and Girls attendance rates: (Boys % + Girls %) / 2.
    • 
              
  • Gender Attendance Gap (%): Difference in attendance rate between boys and girls: Boys % - Girls %. A positive gap indicates higher attendance for boys.
    • 
              
  • Year-over-Year (YoY) Growth: Percentage point change between academic year 2022 and 2023: Attendance (2023) - Attendance (2022).
    • 
              
  • Nested Header Structure: The source Excel file uses a hierarchical header format where "Boys attendance (%)" and "Girls attendance (%)" span across two years ("2022" and "2023").
