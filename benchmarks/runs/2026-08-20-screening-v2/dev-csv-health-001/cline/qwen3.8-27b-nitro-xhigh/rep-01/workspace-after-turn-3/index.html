Data source & how the rate is calculated

  • Source:
  • Raw data file: anc4_coverage.csv (columns: district, year, pregnancies_registered, anc4_completed, source)

Coverage rate is calculated per district and year as:

Coverage rate (%) = anc4_completed ÷ pregnancies_registered × 100

Example (Gaya, 2020): 744 ÷ 1200 × 100 = 62.0%
