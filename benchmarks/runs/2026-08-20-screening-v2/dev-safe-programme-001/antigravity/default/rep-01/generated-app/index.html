Core Evaluation Formulas & Calculation Standards

ISO/TVET-2025 Metric Standards
Formula 1: Completion Rate Primary Metric

Proportion of enrolled female trainees who complete course curriculum requirements.

Completion Rate (%) = Women Completed Training Total Women Enrolled × 100
Formula 2: 6M Employment Rate Tracer Metric

Proportion of certified graduates actively employed at the 6-month post-training tracer milestone.

Employment Rate (6M) = Women Employed after 6M Women Completed Training × 100
Efficiency: Overall Placement Yield Pipeline Yield

End-to-end program conversion from initial enrollment directly to verifiable 6-month employment.

Overall Placement Yield = Women Employed after 6M Total Women Enrolled × 100

Official Data Source, Census Citation & Verification Protocol

Longitudinal tracer study governance, auditing standards, and citations.

Publishing Authority & Division: Department of Labor & Women's Skills Empowerment Division
Formal Publication Title: National Longitudinal Tracer Study on Female Vocational Trainees (2021–2025)
Formal Academic / Policy Citation:
Women TVET Outcome Observatory (2025). Longitudinal Tracer Survey of Training Completion and 6-Month Employment Rates by District and Fiscal Year. Open Gov Data Portal.
Tracer Methodology & 6-Month Protocol:

Multi-point verification: Trainee cohort outcomes are audited exactly 180 days (6 calendar months) post-certification through employer payroll registers, self-employment tax filings, and biometric verification logs.

Total Audited Cohort N = 56,120 Trainees
Confidence Level 95% (MoE ±1.2%)
