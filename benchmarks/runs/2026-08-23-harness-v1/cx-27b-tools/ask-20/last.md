

Saswad led with the most field visits, followed by Rajgurunagar and a tie between Daund and Narayangaon.

| Village | Visits |
|---|---|
| Saswad | 26 |
| Rajgurunagar | 24 |
| Daund | 20 |
| Narayangaon | 20 |
| Baramati | 19 |
| Shirur | 19 |

The remaining 14 villages had between 8 and 18 visits each.
