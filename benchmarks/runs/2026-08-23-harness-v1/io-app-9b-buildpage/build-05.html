The dashboard visualizes the donor journey from initial contact to conversion, alongside the financial impact of specific campaigns. The funnel chart shows the volume of leads at each stage, highlighting where potential donors drop off. The revenue bars break down the actual money captured per campaign, allowing for performance comparison. The line chart tracks daily revenue flow to identify peak donation days. Finally, the table lists the top individual donors contributing to the 80G receipts.

Funnel: Lead Status

How this was computed · 6 rows
SELECT "Lead Status" AS status, COUNT(*) AS cnt FROM donor_crm_zoho_export GROUP BY 1 ORDER BY CASE "Lead Status" WHEN 'New' THEN 1 WHEN 'Contacted' THEN 2 WHEN 'Qualified' THEN 3 WHEN 'Converted' THEN 4 END

Revenue by Campaign

How this was computed · 6 rows
WITH raw AS (SELECT "notes.campaign" AS campaign, SUM(CAST(amount / 100.0 AS DOUBLE)) AS revenue FROM razorpay_payments WHERE status = 'captured' GROUP BY 1) SELECT campaign, revenue FROM raw ORDER BY revenue DESC LIMIT 10
