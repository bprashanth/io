This dashboard tracks the cumulative progress of rural development initiatives from April to September 2025. The top KPIs reveal the total scale of impact across all villages, showing the aggregate number of households reached, Self Help Groups formed, water structures built, and farmers trained.

Trend lines illustrate the month-over-month velocity of these activities. You can observe how the pace of household outreach, SHG formation, and infrastructure development evolved throughout the monsoon season, highlighting periods of acceleration or delay.

The final table ranks the most active villages, allowing stakeholders to identify top performers and those requiring additional support to meet targets.
