The dashboard tracks field activities through two primary data streams: WhatsApp messages from the field group and the official village visit log. The first panel shows the cumulative count of children reported in WhatsApp messages containing keywords like 'bacche' or 'aaye', providing a real-time pulse on community engagement.

The line chart visualizes the daily trend of children reached, allowing the coordinator to spot spikes or drops in activity over time. This helps in understanding the rhythm of field operations and identifying days with higher outreach efforts.

For location-based insights, the bar chart ranks villages by the number of visits conducted, highlighting the most active areas. The accompanying table details the total households met in the top five villages, offering a deeper look into the scale of operations in key locations.

Finally, the staff performance and purpose distribution panels break down who is doing the work and why. This ensures that resource allocation is balanced and that all critical objectives, from health camps to grievance redressal, are being addressed effectively across the region.
