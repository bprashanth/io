This dashboard tracks maternal health interventions through two primary channels: antenatal care (ANC) visits and community health camps. The ANC funnel visualizes the drop-off rate from the first scheduled visit (ANC1) through the final check-up (ANC4), highlighting where engagement might be lost. The KPIs provide a high-level summary of total approved records, average maternal age, and the proportion of women planning institutional deliveries.

Community engagement is measured via the camp register, which lists attendance by village and gender. The bar charts break down these demographics to identify which villages have the highest participation and the gender balance of attendees. The table panel lists the top ten villages by the number of ANC records, allowing for a quick comparison of health service uptake across different locations.

By combining data from the Kobo export and the camp register, we can see the full picture of maternal health outreach. The narrative placeholders allow for specific insights, such as noting that [not computed] women started the program, while only [not computed] completed the full series. Similarly, camp attendance shows [not computed] had the highest turnout, with [not computed] being the most frequent attendee.

ANC Visit Funnel

How this was computed · 4 rows
WITH funnel AS (SELECT 'ANC1' AS stage, COUNT(*) AS cnt FROM "anc_tracking_kobo_export" WHERE "_validation_status" = 'validation_status_approved' UNION ALL SELECT 'ANC2', COUNT(*) FROM "anc_tracking_kobo_export" WHERE "_validation_status" = 'validation_status_approved' AND "visit/anc2" = 'yes' UNION ALL SELECT 'ANC3', COUNT(*) FROM "anc_tracking_kobo_export" WHERE "_validation_status" = 'validation_status_approved' AND "visit/anc3" = 'yes' UNION ALL SELECT 'ANC4', COUNT(*) FROM "anc_tracking_kobo_export" WHERE "_validation_status" = 'validation_status_approved' AND "visit/anc4" = 'yes') SELECT stage, cnt FROM funnel ORDER BY stage
