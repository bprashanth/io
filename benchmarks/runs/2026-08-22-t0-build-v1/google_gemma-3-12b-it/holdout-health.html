This dashboard provides a snapshot of key health indicators across districts. The 'Total Households Surveyed' KPI gives an overview of the data coverage. Bar charts illustrate the average literacy rates among women aged 15-49 and the percentage of children fully vaccinated, allowing for comparisons between districts. The 'Stunted Children Under 5' bar chart highlights areas needing focused intervention. A table shows sanitation access, and a line chart tracks vaccination trends over survey rounds. Further investigation into districts with low literacy or high stunting rates is recommended, using the data to inform targeted programs and resource allocation. The overall health status, as reflected by 82,600, is a key indicator of community well-being.

Literate Women (15-49)

How this was computed · 15 rows
SELECT "District", AVG("Women age 15-49 years who are literate (%)") AS literacy FROM district_health_factsheet GROUP BY 1 ORDER BY 2 DESC LIMIT 15

Fully Vaccinated Children

How this was computed · 15 rows
SELECT "District", AVG("Children age 12-23 months fully vaccinated (%)") AS vaccination FROM district_health_factsheet GROUP BY 1 ORDER BY 2 DESC LIMIT 15

Stunted Children Under 5

How this was computed · 15 rows
SELECT "District", AVG("Children under 5 years who are stunted (%)") AS stunting FROM district_health_factsheet GROUP BY 1 ORDER BY 2 ASC LIMIT 15
