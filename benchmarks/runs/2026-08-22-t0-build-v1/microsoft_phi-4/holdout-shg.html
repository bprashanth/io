SHG Loan Repayment Report

Overview of SHG loan repayment status and purposes

This report provides insights into the repayment status of SHG loans. The pie chart (60) illustrates the distribution of loans across different repayment statuses: Closed, Overdue, and Regular. The bar chart (106) shows the number of loans disbursed for various purposes such as Agriculture, Education, Health, Housing, Livestock, and Petty trade. Additionally, the bar chart (18) focuses on overdue loans, highlighting the purposes for which these loans were taken. This information can help in understanding the areas where financial support is most needed and where repayment challenges are prevalent.
