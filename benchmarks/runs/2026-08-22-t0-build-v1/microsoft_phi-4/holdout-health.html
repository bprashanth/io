District Health Dashboard

Comparative analysis of key health indicators across districts

Total Households Surveyed
82,600 households

This dashboard provides a comparative analysis of key health indicators across various districts. The literacy rate among women aged 15-49 years is shown in panel 74.05, highlighting the average literacy percentage per district. Panel 73.65 displays the vaccination rate for children aged 12-23 months, indicating the percentage of fully vaccinated children. Institutional births, shown in panel 89.6, reflect the percentage of births occurring in medical facilities. The stunting rate in children under 5 years is presented in panel 48.15, providing insights into nutritional and health challenges. Finally, panel 65.3 illustrates the percentage of households with improved sanitation facilities, an important indicator of public health infrastructure.

Literacy Rate Among Women (15-49 years)

Showing the first 20 of 21 District values.

How this was computed · 21 rows
SELECT "District", AVG("Women age 15-49 years who are literate (%)") AS avg_literacy FROM district_health_factsheet GROUP BY 1 ORDER BY 2 DESC
