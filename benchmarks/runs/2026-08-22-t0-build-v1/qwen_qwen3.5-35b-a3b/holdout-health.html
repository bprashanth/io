This dashboard provides a comparative view of health outcomes across 20 districts. The average literacy rate among women aged 15-49 is 57.48, indicating the baseline for health awareness in the region. Vaccination coverage for children aged 12-23 months stands at 59.79 on average, while institutional births account for 74.66 of all deliveries. The bar charts highlight significant variations in vaccination and stunting rates between districts, with the highest vaccination rates shown in the top performing areas. The summary table offers a detailed breakdown of these metrics for every district, allowing for targeted interventions where stunting or low literacy rates are most prevalent.

Vaccination Rate by District

How this was computed · 15 rows
SELECT "District", AVG("Children age 12-23 months fully vaccinated (%)") AS avg_vaccination FROM "district_health_factsheet" WHERE "District" != 'State Total' GROUP BY 1 ORDER BY 2 DESC LIMIT 15

How this was computed · 15 rows

How this was computed · 20 rows
SELECT "District", ROUND(AVG("Women age 15-49 years who are literate (%)"), 1) AS avg_literacy, ROUND(AVG("Children age 12-23 months fully vaccinated (%)"), 1) AS avg_vaccination, ROUND(AVG("Institutional births (%)"), 1) AS avg_births, ROUND(AVG("Children under 5 years who are stunted (%)"), 1) AS avg_stunting FROM "district_health_factsheet" WHERE "District" != 'State Total' GROUP BY 1 ORDER BY avg_vaccination DESC
