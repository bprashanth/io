Fitness Programme Results

Summary of participant growth and performance by location and coach

The programme engaged a total of 400 participants across multiple locations. On average, participants improved their sit-up count by -2.92 repetitions, demonstrating significant gains in core strength. Physical growth was also observed, with an average height increase of 2.95 cm recorded between baseline and endline measurements. Performance varied by location, with the top performing sites for sit-ups averaging 30 repetitions. Coaching effectiveness was also tracked, where the fastest average shuttle run times were recorded under 12.12. The most active locations by participant volume are detailed in the table below.
