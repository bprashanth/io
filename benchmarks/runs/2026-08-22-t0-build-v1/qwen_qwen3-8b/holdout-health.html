This dashboard provides an overview of key health indicators across districts. 21 districts were surveyed in the latest round. The average literacy rate among women aged 15-49 is 76.5, while the average vaccination rate for children aged 12-23 months is 75.5. Institutional births account for 95.1 on average, and the stunting rate among children under 5 is 49.6. Sanitation facilities are available in 77.5 of households.
