The dashboard provides a quick snapshot of the scholarship program's reach and performance. The total number of applicants and the count of approved applications give a clear sense of participation and approval rates. Average marks and average family income help gauge the academic standing and economic background of the cohort. Bar charts break down the distribution across status, category, and taluka, highlighting where resources are concentrated and where outreach may be needed. Finally, the monthly trend line shows how applications are evolving over time, while the top‑10 table spotlights the highest‑scoring candidates for further recognition.
