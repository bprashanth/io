How this was computed · 2 rows

Average score by site

How this was computed · 10 rows
SELECT "School Name" AS site, AVG("Girls 2022" + "Girls 2023" + "Girls 2024" + "Boys 2022" + "Boys 2023" + "Boys 2024") / 6 AS avg_score FROM "school_enrolment_by_year" GROUP BY 1 ORDER BY 2 DESC LIMIT 10
