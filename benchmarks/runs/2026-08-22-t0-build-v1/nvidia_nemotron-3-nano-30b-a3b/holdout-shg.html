SHG Loan Register Overview

Repayment status, overdue loans, and purpose distribution

The dashboard shows the scale and composition of SHG loan activity in the district. The total number of loans disbursed provides a baseline for understanding the program's reach. Loan purpose distribution highlights where financial resources are being allocated, while the repayment status breakdown reveals the health of the loan portfolio. Finally, the top overdue loans list helps identify potential risk areas that may require targeted intervention.
