This dashboard provides a concise overview of district-level health metrics. The KPI shows the total households surveyed across all districts, giving a sense of data coverage. The bar chart ranks districts by average female literacy, highlighting regional disparities. The line chart tracks monthly changes in household survey volume, revealing trends in data collection. Finally, the table lists the top 10 districts by literacy rate, allowing quick identification of high-performing areas.

Average score by site

Showing the first 20 of 21 site values.

How this was computed · 21 rows
SELECT "District" AS "site", AVG("Women age 15-49 years who are literate (%)") AS "avg_score" FROM "district_health_factsheet" GROUP BY "District" ORDER BY "avg_score" DESC

How this was computed · 2 rows
