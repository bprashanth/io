Average score by site

How this was computed · 10 rows
SELECT "village" AS "site", AVG("q01") AS "avg_score" FROM "household_survey" GROUP BY "village" ORDER BY "avg_score" DESC LIMIT 10

Monthly trend

How this was computed · 6 rows
SELECT DATE_TRUNC('month', CAST("_submission_time" AS DATE)) AS "month", SUM("q01") AS "total" FROM "household_survey" GROUP BY "month" ORDER BY "month"
