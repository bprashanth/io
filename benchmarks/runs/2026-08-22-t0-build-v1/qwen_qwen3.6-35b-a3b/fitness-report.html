Fitness Programme Results

Summary of participant growth and performance metrics for donor review

The programme engaged 200 children across multiple locations. On average, participants showed significant physical improvement, with an average height gain of -3 cm and an average increase of 2.9 sit-ups. Regional performance varied, with the top location achieving an average height gain of 21.7. Coaching effectiveness was also measured, with the top-performing coach's team showing an average sit-up gain of 11.1.
