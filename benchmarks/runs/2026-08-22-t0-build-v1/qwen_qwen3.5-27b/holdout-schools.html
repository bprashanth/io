The dashboard shows a total of 9,343 students enrolled in 2024, with girls making up 52.4% of the student population. This indicates progress toward gender parity in education.

Over the years, enrolment trends reveal steady growth for both genders, though the gap between boys and girls remains a focus area for intervention.

Infrastructure gaps persist, with only 25 schools having functional toilets for girls. Access to drinking water and electricity also varies significantly across schools.

Addressing these gaps is critical to improving retention and learning outcomes, especially for girls who may face additional barriers due to inadequate facilities.

How this was computed · 3 rows
WITH yearly_totals AS (
  SELECT 
    '2022' AS year,
    SUM(COALESCE("Girls 2022", 0)) AS girls,
    SUM(COALESCE("Boys 2022", 0)) AS boys
  FROM "school_enrolment_by_year"
  UNION ALL
  SELECT 
    '2023' AS year,
    SUM(COALESCE("Girls 2023", 0)) AS girls,
    SUM(COALESCE("Boys 2023", 0)) AS boys
  FROM "school_enrolment_by_year"
  UNION ALL
  SELECT 
    '2024' AS year,
    SUM(COALESCE("Girls 2024", 0)) AS girls,
    SUM(COALESCE("Boys 2024", 0)) AS boys
  FROM "school_enrolment_by_year"
)
SELECT year, girls, boys FROM yearly_totals ORDER BY year

How this was computed · 20 rows
SELECT 
  "School Code",
  CASE WHEN "Toilets functional (girls)" = 'No' THEN 1 ELSE 0 END AS missing_toilets,
  CASE WHEN "Drinking water" = 'No' THEN 1 ELSE 0 END AS missing_water,
  CASE WHEN "Electricity" = 'No' THEN 1 ELSE 0 END AS missing_electricity
FROM "school_infrastructure"
ORDER BY missing_toilets DESC, missing_water DESC, missing_electricity DESC
LIMIT 20
