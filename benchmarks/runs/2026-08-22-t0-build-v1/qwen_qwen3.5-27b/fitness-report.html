Fitness Programme Impact Summary

Overview of participant growth and performance changes from baseline to endline

This programme engaged 200 unique children across multiple locations. The data shows a clear trend in physical fitness improvements.

On average, participants improved their shuttle run times by -0.01 seconds, indicating better speed and agility. Simultaneously, core strength increased with an average gain of 2.92 sit-ups per child.

Performance varies by coach, with some groups showing faster shuttle run times and higher sit-up counts than others. The top performers list highlights individuals who made the most significant strides in both metrics.
