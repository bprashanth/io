This dashboard provides a comparative view of health outcomes across 40 districts. The average rate of institutional births stands at 7,466, while the average child vaccination rate is 5,979.

The bar charts highlight disparities between districts, showing which areas lead in institutional deliveries and which struggle with child stunting. These visualizations help identify priority zones for intervention.

For a granular view, the detailed table lists specific metrics including sanitation facilities and literacy rates, allowing stakeholders to drill down into the data for each district.

Institutional Births by District

How this was computed · 20 rows
SELECT "District" AS district, "Institutional births (%)" * 100 AS inst_births FROM "district_health_factsheet" WHERE "District" != 'State Total' ORDER BY inst_births DESC LIMIT 20

Child Stunting Rates by District

How this was computed · 20 rows
SELECT "District" AS district, "Children under 5 years who are stunted (%)" * 100 AS stunting_rate FROM "district_health_factsheet" WHERE "District" != 'State Total' ORDER BY stunting_rate DESC LIMIT 20

How this was computed · 40 rows
