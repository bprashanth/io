Gender Comparison of Improvement

Average sit-up count improvement from baseline to endline by gender

The dashboard presents a single bar chart comparing the average improvement in sit-up counts between girls and boys. The improvement is calculated as the difference between the endline and baseline sit-up counts for each child. The chart allows a direct visual comparison of whether girls and boys are improving equally. The numeric value shown for each gender can be referenced as 3.45 for females and 3.45 for males, indicating the magnitude of improvement for each group.

Average Sit-ups Improvement by Gender

How this was computed · 2 rows
SELECT e.Gender, AVG(e."Sit ups" - b."Sit ups") AS avg_improvement FROM endline e JOIN baseline b ON e."Child ID" = b."Child ID" GROUP BY 1 ORDER BY avg_improvement DESC
