Household Survey Coverage

Village and enumerator coverage, and submission trends over time

The dashboard provides an overview of household survey coverage across villages and enumerators, as well as the temporal trend of submissions. Village coverage (9) highlights the most frequently surveyed locations, while enumerator coverage (18) identifies the most active fieldworkers. The time series (1) reveals patterns in data collection over the reporting period.

Village coverage

How this was computed · 20 rows
SELECT village, COUNT(*) AS n FROM household_survey GROUP BY 1 ORDER BY n DESC LIMIT 20

Enumerator coverage

How this was computed · 10 rows
SELECT enumerator_name, COUNT(*) AS n FROM household_survey GROUP BY 1 ORDER BY n DESC LIMIT 20

Submissions over time

How this was computed · 97 rows
SELECT DATE(_submission_time) AS month, COUNT(*) AS n FROM household_survey GROUP BY 1 ORDER BY 1
