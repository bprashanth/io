School Dashboard

Enrolment trends, infrastructure status, and gender comparison

The dashboard provides a comprehensive view of school performance. Panel 85 and 168 illustrate the enrolment trends for girls and boys across 2022, 2023, and 2024, allowing educators to track growth or decline over time. Panel 1.01 Cr highlights critical infrastructure gaps, such as non-functional toilets for girls, lack of drinking water, and electricity shortages, which are essential for creating a conducive learning environment. Finally, panel 1.24 presents the girls-to-boys enrolment ratio per block, expressed as a percentage, to assess gender parity in school participation. Together, these visualizations support data-driven decision-making for improving educational outcomes and infrastructure quality.

How this was computed · 40 rows

How this was computed · 24 rows

Girls vs Boys enrolment ratio

How this was computed · 4 rows
SELECT "Block", SUM("Girls 2024") * 1.0 / NULLIF(SUM("Boys 2024"), 0) AS ratio FROM school_enrolment_by_year GROUP BY "Block" ORDER BY ratio DESC
