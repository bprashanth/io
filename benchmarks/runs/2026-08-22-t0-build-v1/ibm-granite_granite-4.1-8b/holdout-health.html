District Health Dashboard

Comparing key health indicators across districts

The dashboard presents four key health indicators for each district, allowing a quick visual comparison. 76.8 shows the percentage of children aged 12-23 months who are fully vaccinated, highlighting districts with higher immunization coverage. 49.6 displays the stunting rate among children under five, identifying areas with greater nutritional challenges. 76.5 reveals literacy rates among women aged 15-49, indicating educational attainment levels. Finally, 95.1 measures institutional birth rates, reflecting access to healthcare services during childbirth. Together, these panels provide a comprehensive snapshot of district-level health performance.
