Scholarship Scheme Progress by District (Pending Cases)

Shows the number of pending scholarship applications per district and scheme.

The dashboard provides an overview of scholarship scheme progress, focusing on pending cases. Panel p1 displays a bar chart of pending applications by district, highlighting which districts have the highest backlog. Panel p2 lists the top five districts with the most pending applications, offering a quick reference for targeted interventions. Panel p3 breaks down pending applications by scholarship scheme, allowing administrators to identify which programs require immediate attention. Finally, panel p4 presents a pie chart showing the distribution of pending applications across different applicant categories, helping to understand demographic aspects of the backlog. All numeric values are represented using placeholders such as 25 for the first numeric cell in each panel, ensuring the narrative remains flexible and data-driven.
