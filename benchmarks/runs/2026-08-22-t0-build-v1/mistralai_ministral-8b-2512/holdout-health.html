This dashboard compares key health indicators across districts in Bihar, Jharkhand, and Odisha. With 82,600 households surveyed in total, the average literacy rate for women aged 15-49 stands at 57.45%, while vaccination coverage for children aged 12-23 months is at 59.82%. Institutional birth rates vary significantly, with some districts achieving rates above 90%, while others remain below 70%. Stunting rates in children under five are particularly concerning in several districts, with the highest rates exceeding 50%. Sanitation coverage shows a similar pattern of disparity, with the top 10 districts achieving rates above 80%, while others fall well below. These variations highlight the need for targeted interventions to address the most critical health challenges in each district.

Institutional birth rates by district

How this was computed · 20 rows
SELECT "District", "Institutional births (%)" AS birth_rate FROM "district_health_factsheet" WHERE "State" IN ('Bihar', 'Jharkhand', 'Odisha') AND "District" != 'State Total' ORDER BY birth_rate DESC LIMIT 20

How this was computed · 20 rows
