The dashboard highlights key health metrics across districts. The first three KPIs show overall household coverage, female literacy, and child vaccination rates. Bar charts compare institutional birth rates and stunting prevalence, revealing districts that excel or lag. The table lists the top ten districts by female literacy, providing a quick reference for targeted interventions. All figures are averages across survey rounds, ensuring comparability.

Institutional births by district

How this was computed · 20 rows
SELECT "District" AS district, AVG("Institutional births (%)") AS institutional_births FROM "district_health_factsheet" GROUP BY 1 ORDER BY 2 DESC LIMIT 20

Stunting among children under 5 by district

How this was computed · 20 rows
SELECT "District" AS district, AVG("Children under 5 years who are stunted (%)") AS stunting FROM "district_health_factsheet" GROUP BY 1 ORDER BY 2 DESC LIMIT 20
