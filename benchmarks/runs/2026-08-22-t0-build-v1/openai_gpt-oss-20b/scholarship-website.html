The dashboard shows the overall progress of scholarship schemes across districts. The first KPI displays the total number of applicants. The bar chart on the left illustrates the approval rate per district, highlighting which areas have the highest success rates. The right bar chart focuses on pending cases, indicating districts where more applicants are awaiting decision. Finally, the table lists the top 10 pending applicants, providing quick access to individual records for follow‑up. All figures are dynamic and update as new data is entered into the system.

Approval Rate by District

How this was computed · 3 rows
SELECT "District" AS district, ROUND(100.0*NULLIF(SUM(CASE WHEN "Status"='Approved' THEN 1 END),0)/NULLIF(COUNT(*),0),2) AS approval_rate FROM scholarship_applicants GROUP BY 1 ORDER BY 2 DESC LIMIT 8

Pending Cases by District

How this was computed · 3 rows
SELECT "District" AS district, COUNT(*) AS pending_cases FROM scholarship_applicants WHERE "Status"='Pending' GROUP BY 1 ORDER BY 2 DESC LIMIT 8
