The dashboard provides a concise view of the scholarship applicant pool. The first four KPIs show the overall size of the applicant base, the number of approvals, the average academic performance, and the average family income, giving a quick snapshot of the demographic and academic profile. The bar chart on status distribution highlights how many applicants are in each stage of the process, helping to identify bottlenecks or areas needing attention. The second bar chart breaks down average marks by category, revealing which groups are performing best academically. Finally, the table lists the top 10 applicants by marks, allowing stakeholders to quickly spot high performers and their associated talukas and statuses. All numeric values are represented by placeholders such as 300, 48, 67.17, 1.29 L, 76, 71.54, and 97.5 to keep the data dynamic and up‑to‑date.
