This dashboard aggregates household survey data to highlight key health metrics across selected districts. The first panel shows the total number of households included in the survey, providing context for the rates below.

The subsequent panels break down specific indicators: vaccination coverage for children aged 12-23 months, literacy rates among women aged 15-49, and stunting rates among children under five. Each bar chart is limited to the top 10 districts by the respective metric to ensure readability and focus on the most significant variations.

Additional metrics include the percentage of institutional births and households with improved sanitation facilities. By comparing these six indicators side-by-side, users can quickly identify which districts are performing well and which require targeted intervention.
