The dashboard highlights the total student population in 2024, which is the sum of girls and boys enrolled across all schools. By comparing the enrolment trends from 2022 to 2024, we can observe the growth trajectory for both genders. The bar chart specifically isolates the 2024 figures to visualize the current gender gap in attendance.

How this was computed · 2 rows
WITH yearly_totals AS (SELECT 'Girls' AS series, SUM("Girls 2022") AS g22, SUM("Girls 2023") AS g23, SUM("Girls 2024") AS g24 FROM "school_enrolment_by_year" WHERE "School Code" IS NOT NULL UNION ALL SELECT 'Boys' AS series, SUM("Boys 2022") AS g22, SUM("Boys 2023") AS g23, SUM("Boys 2024") AS g24 FROM "school_enrolment_by_year" WHERE "School Code" IS NOT NULL) SELECT series, g22, g23, g24 FROM yearly_totals ORDER BY series, g24 DESC
