Gender Gap Analysis

Comparing improvement in physical fitness metrics between girls and boys

Total participants
200

This dashboard compares the average improvement in sit-ups, shuttle run times, and height between girls and boys. The first bar chart shows the average gain in sit-ups, while the second displays the change in shuttle run seconds (where a negative value indicates improvement). The third panel tracks height growth for both groups.

Detailed tables list the top five performers for each gender based on sit-ups improvement. These tables help identify specific individuals who have shown the most significant gains in strength and endurance.

By comparing the average differences across the bar charts, you can determine if one gender is outperforming the other in specific metrics. The data allows for a direct comparison of progress rates between the two groups.
