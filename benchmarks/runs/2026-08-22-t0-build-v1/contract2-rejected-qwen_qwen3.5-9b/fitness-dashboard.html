This dashboard tracks the physical progress of children from baseline to endline measurements. The first panel shows the total number of unique children recorded in the dataset. Subsequent bar charts break down the average improvement in height, sit-ups, and shuttle run times by location and coach, highlighting which groups saw the most significant gains. The tables list the individual children with the highest improvements in height and sit-ups, providing specific examples of success.
