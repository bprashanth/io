This dashboard provides a comparative view of health outcomes across districts. The average full vaccination rate for children aged 12-23 months is 59.82, while the average stunting rate among children under 5 is 37.82. Districts vary significantly in these metrics, as shown in the bar charts. For a detailed breakdown by survey round, refer to the table.

Vaccination Rate by District

How this was computed · 20 rows
SELECT "District", AVG("Children age 12-23 months fully vaccinated (%)") AS avg_vaccination FROM "district_health_factsheet" WHERE "District" != 'State Total' GROUP BY 1 ORDER BY 2 DESC

How this was computed · 40 rows
SELECT "District", "Survey Round", AVG("Women age 15-49 years who are literate (%)") AS avg_literacy, AVG("Children age 12-23 months fully vaccinated (%)") AS avg_vaccination, AVG("Institutional births (%)") AS avg_institutional_births, AVG("Children under 5 years who are stunted (%)") AS avg_stunting, AVG("Households with improved sanitation facility (%)") AS avg_sanitation FROM "district_health_factsheet" WHERE "District" != 'State Total' GROUP BY 1, 2 ORDER BY 1, 2
