Scholarship Application Overview

Summary of applicants, approval rates, and funding distribution by scheme

The dashboard presents a comprehensive view of the scholarship application lifecycle, starting with the total volume of submissions received. It breaks down the current status of these applications, highlighting how many have been approved, are still pending review, or have been rejected. This allows stakeholders to quickly gauge the efficiency of the processing pipeline.

Demographic analysis reveals the distribution of applicants across different social categories and specific scholarship schemes. By visualizing these segments, the board can identify which groups are most engaged and which schemes are currently the most popular among the student population.

Temporal trends show the flow of applications over time, helping to identify peak submission periods. Additionally, a geographic breakdown pinpoints the villages with the highest concentration of applicants, ensuring that resource allocation and outreach efforts can be targeted effectively to areas with high demand.
