This dashboard provides a comprehensive overview of the scholarship scheme's progress across the three districts: Gaya, Muzaffarpur, and Pune. The top row highlights key metrics, including the total number of applications received, the count of pending cases requiring attention, the number of approved applications, and the total disbursed amount based on family income data.

The bar charts break down the data geographically and by status. The first chart shows the volume of applications per district, helping to identify areas with high demand. The second chart specifically isolates pending cases by district, allowing administrators to prioritize regions with significant backlogs. The pie chart visualizes the distribution of applications across different scholarship schemes, such as the EBC Scholarship Scheme and the Merit-cum-Means Scholarship.

Finally, the table lists the top pending cases by serial number, providing a quick reference for the names of applicants, their respective districts, and their family income. This granular view supports targeted follow-up actions to clear the pending queue efficiently.
