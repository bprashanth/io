This dashboard provides a comprehensive view of the scholarship applicant pool, highlighting key metrics such as total volume, average academic performance, and approval rates. The top section aggregates the total number of applicants, the mean marks percentage, and the specific counts for approved and pending cases to give an immediate snapshot of the program's current state.

The middle section breaks down the data by categorical variables to identify trends. You can observe the distribution of applicants across different statuses to see where bottlenecks might exist, and compare average marks across various categories like General, OBC, and SC/ST to ensure equitable academic outcomes. Additionally, the regional analysis visualizes the concentration of applicants by Taluka and District, revealing which areas have the highest participation.

Finally, the detailed table lists the top 10 applicants based on their marks percentage, providing a quick reference for high-performing candidates. This granular view complements the aggregated charts, allowing stakeholders to drill down into specific records for further review or verification.
