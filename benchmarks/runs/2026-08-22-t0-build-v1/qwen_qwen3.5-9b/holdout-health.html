This dashboard aggregates health metrics from the district factsheet to provide a comparative view of key indicators. The top row displays the total number of districts analyzed alongside the average rates for vaccination, literacy, and institutional births across the region.

The bar charts below break down these averages by individual district, allowing for a granular comparison of performance. Vaccination coverage and literacy rates are sorted in descending order to highlight the best-performing areas, while sanitation facilities are similarly ranked to identify gaps in infrastructure.

The final table provides a comprehensive view of all metrics for every district, including stunting rates which are not featured in the summary KPIs. This allows stakeholders to cross-reference high vaccination rates with other critical health outcomes like stunting and sanitation access.

Vaccination by District

How this was computed · 10 rows
SELECT "District", "Children age 12-23 months fully vaccinated (%)" AS "vacc_rate" FROM "district_health_factsheet" WHERE "District" != 'State Total' ORDER BY "vacc_rate" DESC LIMIT 10

Literacy by District

How this was computed · 10 rows
SELECT "District", "Women age 15-49 years who are literate (%)" AS "lit_rate" FROM "district_health_factsheet" WHERE "District" != 'State Total' ORDER BY "lit_rate" DESC LIMIT 10

Sanitation by District

How this was computed · 10 rows
