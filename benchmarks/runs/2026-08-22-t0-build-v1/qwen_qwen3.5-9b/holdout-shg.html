SHG Loan Repayment & Purpose

Overview of loan disbursements, repayment status, and usage across blocks

This dashboard provides a comprehensive view of the Self-Help Group (SHG) loan register, focusing on repayment health and the intended use of funds. The top section highlights key financial metrics, including the total value of loans disbursed, the cumulative amount repaid, the total number of members involved, and the specific sum currently marked as overdue. These figures offer an immediate snapshot of the financial scale and liquidity of the SHG network.

The middle section breaks down the loan portfolio by repayment status and purpose. A bar chart illustrates the distribution of loans across categories such as Regular, Overdue, and Closed, helping to identify potential risks in the portfolio. Another chart details the primary reasons for borrowing, such as Agriculture, Education, or Health, revealing the economic priorities of the member groups.

Finally, a table lists the top five blocks by total loan volume, providing a geographic perspective on where the majority of financial activity is concentrated. This allows stakeholders to target interventions or support resources to the most active or potentially vulnerable regions within the network.
