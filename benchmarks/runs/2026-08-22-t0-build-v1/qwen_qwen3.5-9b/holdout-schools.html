This dashboard provides a snapshot of the educational landscape, focusing on enrolment figures and infrastructure quality. The top KPIs reveal the total number of schools, the aggregate count of girls and boys enrolled, and the proportion of schools equipped with electricity.

The bar charts illustrate the growth trajectory of enrolment for both genders over the last three years, allowing for a direct comparison of trends at the individual school level. The pie chart visualizes the availability of critical infrastructure, specifically electricity, drinking water, and functional toilets for girls, highlighting potential gaps in resource distribution.

Finally, the table lists the top ten schools by girls' enrolment, providing a detailed view of the most successful institutions in terms of female participation. This data helps identify high-performing schools and areas where infrastructure investment might be most needed to support continued growth.

Infrastructure Availability

How this was computed · 8 rows
SELECT "School Code", SUM(CASE WHEN "Electricity" = 'Yes' THEN 1 ELSE 0 END) AS has_elec, SUM(CASE WHEN "Drinking water" = 'Yes' THEN 1 ELSE 0 END) AS has_water, SUM(CASE WHEN "Toilets functional (girls)" = 'Yes' THEN 1 ELSE 0 END) AS has_toilets FROM "school_infrastructure" GROUP BY 1 ORDER BY has_elec DESC LIMIT 8
