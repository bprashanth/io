A total of 200 children completed both baseline and endline assessments. Overall muscular endurance improved notably, with an average increase of 2.92 sit-ups per participant, while agility and sprint speed improved with an average reduction of 0.01 seconds in the shuttle run test.

Performance improvements across coaching cohorts and geographic locations highlight the varying rates of development, providing actionable insights for targeted athletic support and coaching best practices.

Average Sit-ups Gain by Coach

How this was computed · 15 rows
SELECT b."Coach" AS coach, ROUND(AVG(e."Sit ups" - b."Sit ups"), 2) AS avg_situps_gain FROM "baseline" b JOIN "endline" e ON b."Child ID" = e."Child ID" GROUP BY 1 ORDER BY 2 DESC LIMIT 15

How this was computed · 15 rows

How this was computed · 45 rows
SELECT b."Location" AS location, COUNT(DISTINCT b."Child ID") AS total_children, ROUND(AVG(e."Sit ups" - b."Sit ups"), 2) AS avg_situp_gain, ROUND(AVG(b."Shuttle run sec" - e."Shuttle run sec"), 2) AS avg_shuttle_improvement_sec, ROUND(AVG(e."Height cm" - b."Height cm"), 2) AS avg_height_growth_cm, ROUND(AVG(e."Weight kg" - b."Weight kg"), 2) AS avg_weight_gain_kg FROM "baseline" b JOIN "endline" e ON b."Child ID" = e."Child ID" GROUP BY 1 ORDER BY avg_situp_gain DESC
