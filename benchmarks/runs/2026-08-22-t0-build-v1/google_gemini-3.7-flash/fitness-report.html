Youth Fitness Programme Impact Report

Measuring physical fitness gains and core strength progression from baseline to endline

A total of 200 children participated in both the baseline and endline assessments across programme locations. The interventions demonstrated tangible gains in both muscular endurance and athletic agility.

Overall, participants increased their core strength performance with an average gain of 2.92 sit-up repetitions. Speed and agility also showed clear progression, with shuttle run times improving by an average reduction of 0.01 seconds.

Performance enhancements were consistent across genders and age categories, underscoring strong training delivery and sustained participant engagement throughout the intervention period.

How this was computed · 10 rows
