Across the reporting schools, total enrolment reached 9,343 students in 2024, with girls representing 52.37% of the student body. Tracking across 2022 to 2024 shows the progress in parity across administrative blocks.

Infrastructure assessments reveal critical basic provisioning, with functional girls' toilets accessible in 62.5% of surveyed facilities. Ensuring all schools achieve comprehensive water, sanitation, and power access remains vital alongside student retention.

Infrastructure Provisioning Rates

How this was computed · 3 rows
SELECT facility, coverage_pct FROM (SELECT 'Functional Girls Toilet' AS facility, 100.0 * COUNT(CASE WHEN "Toilets functional (girls)" = 'Yes' THEN 1 END) / NULLIF(COUNT(*), 0) AS coverage_pct FROM school_infrastructure UNION ALL SELECT 'Drinking Water' AS facility, 100.0 * COUNT(CASE WHEN "Drinking water" = 'Yes' THEN 1 END) / NULLIF(COUNT(*), 0) AS coverage_pct FROM school_infrastructure UNION ALL SELECT 'Electricity' AS facility, 100.0 * COUNT(CASE WHEN "Electricity" = 'Yes' THEN 1 END) / NULLIF(COUNT(*), 0) AS coverage_pct FROM school_infrastructure) ORDER BY coverage_pct DESC
