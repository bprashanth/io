Figures from the 2011 Census of India (Census year: 2011). 2011 is the latest population census carried out in India, so these are the most recent official district population counts.

In plain language

Largest district: Patna (5,838,465 persons = 58.38465 lakh; Census 2011).
Exact difference, Patna − Gaya: 1,447,047 persons (5,838,465 − 4,391,418 = 1,447,047; that is 14.47047 lakh). Patna is larger.

  • Patna has the largest total population of the three: 5,838,465 persons (58.38465 lakh) in 2011.
  • Gaya has 4,391,418 (43.91418 lakh) — that is 1,447,047 fewer than Patna (14.47047 lakh).
  • Nalanda has the smallest total population: 2,877,653 (28.77653 lakh) — that is 1,513,765 fewer than Gaya, and 2,960,812 fewer than Patna.
  • Together the three districts held 13,107,536 persons in 2011 (131.07536 lakh).
