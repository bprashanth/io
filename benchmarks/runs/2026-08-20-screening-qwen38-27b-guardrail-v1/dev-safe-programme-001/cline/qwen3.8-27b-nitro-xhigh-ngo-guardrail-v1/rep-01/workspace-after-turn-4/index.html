Methodology — how the two rates are computed

1 · Training completion rate

Completion rate (%) = (completed_training ÷ women_enrolled) × 100

Share of enrolled women who completed the training.

2 · Employment rate at 6 months

Employment rate (%) = (employed_at_6_months ÷ completed_training) × 100

Share of women who completed the training and were found employed 6 months later. Denominator is the cohort of training completers.
