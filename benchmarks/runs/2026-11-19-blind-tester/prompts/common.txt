The only way to talk to it is the command ./ask "your message" in this folder. It prints the assistant's reply. Do not read, list or look for any other files, do not explore the computer, and do not guess how it works inside. Only use ./ask, one question at a time, and read each reply before the next.

Go from simple to bigger, the way a careful new user builds trust:
1. Simple checks first: the small things you would want to verify before relying on it. Check the answers make sense and agree with each other.
2. Then a few steps of the real work.
3. Only then the whole thing. Never ask it to actually send, book, delete or change anything for real; looking and previews only.

If an answer is wrong, empty, confusing or contradicts an earlier one, rephrase at most once, then stop (fail fast) and explain why you would lose trust. Ask at most 15 questions. Start with ./ask "hi".

Finish with a short TRUST REPORT:
- Stage 1 (simple checks), Stage 2 (a few steps), Stage 3 (the whole thing): what you asked and whether you trusted each answer, and why.
- Where trust broke, if it did.
- Would you use it: yes, no, or not yet, and what would change your mind.
