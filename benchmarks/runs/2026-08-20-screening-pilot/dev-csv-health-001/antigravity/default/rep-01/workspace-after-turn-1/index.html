Transparent Methodology & Calculation

How the ANC-4 Coverage Rate is Calculated

Understanding the mathematical definition, numerator, and denominator

WHO / MOHFW Standard
Mathematical Formula
ANC-4 Coverage Rate (%) =
( anc4_completed ÷ pregnancies_registered ) × 100

The rate expresses the percentage of enrolled pregnant women in a given administrative district who successfully attended a minimum of four antenatal care visits during their pregnancy term.

Components Breakdown
Numerator: anc4_completed Count

The total count of pregnant women in that district & year who received four or more comprehensive antenatal checkups.

Denominator: pregnancies_registered Cohort

The total number of pregnant women registered in the health management information system (HMIS) for that district & year.
