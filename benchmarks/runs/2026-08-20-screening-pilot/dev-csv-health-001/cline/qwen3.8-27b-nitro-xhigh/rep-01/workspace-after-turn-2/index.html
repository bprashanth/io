How the rate is calculated

ANC4+ coverage rate = pregnancies that completed 4+ antenatal checkupspregnancies registered × 100
  • Numerator: anc4_completed — pregnancies with 4 or more antenatal checkups in the selected district and year.
  • Denominator: pregnancies_registered — all pregnancies registered in that district and year.
  • Rates are shown as a percentage, rounded to 1 decimal. "Change vs previous year" is the difference from the previous year's rate, in percentage points.
