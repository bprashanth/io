**What it does**
- Each screenshot becomes its own spreadsheet. The column names are the table's own headings.
- Numbers are written plainly, so 1,204 becomes 1204 and 2,45,600 becomes 245600. That way they add up correctly in a spreadsheet.
- A "check" column shows what to look at:
  - a number it couldn't read clearly;
  - a cell it wasn't sure of;
  - a Total row that doesn't add up. It might have misread something, or the report itself might have a mistake.
- Names and places in the table are hidden as codes before Claude sees it. So you can ask Claude on the right to add up or compare the numbers.

I tested it on three made-up screenshots: a table with lines, one without lines, and a small blurry one. It read all three correctly and caught the wrong total.
