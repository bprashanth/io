**How it works**
- **Listening:** Idli marks every moment of a video (or audio file) as talk, music or quiet. This runs on this computer.
- **Deciding:** Claude sees only that list of times. It never sees or hears the recording.
- **Cutting:** Idli makes a new file whose name ends in "(cut)" with just the talk. It keeps a little space around each bit of talk, and leaves short pauses in so the speech doesn't sound chopped. Your original video stays as it is.
- **Speed:** listening to a two-hour video should take about a minute. Making the new video takes longer for big, sharp videos, roughly 10 to 20 minutes. That is my estimate from a 20-minute test; I haven't timed a real two-hour video.
