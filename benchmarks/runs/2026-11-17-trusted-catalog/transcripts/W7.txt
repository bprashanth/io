[05:51:31] BUILDER: Survey can now turn a photo of a filled-in form into one spreadsheet row.

**What it does**
- The private model on this computer reads the handwriting, in Hindi or English. The photo never leaves this computer.
- Each photo becomes one row with: head of family (in Hindi and in English letters), village (the same), number of members, mobile, monthly income, toilet (Yes or No) and ration card colour.
- It looks at the number boxes a second time. Hindi ४ (4) looks a lot like an English 8. On my test form, the first look read ४००० as 8000 and the second look got 4000 right.
- A **Check** column says "OK" or names the boxes to look at again on the photo. A box goes there if it was hard to read, if it was empty, if the mobile isn't 10 digits, or if the two looks disagreed.

I tested it only on two forms I made up and drew myself. Real handwriting is messier, so the Check column matters most on the first real forms.

**How to try it**
1. Pick a form photo with @ on the left.
2. Look over the row in the privacy check. Names, villages and mobiles become codes before Claude sees them.
3. Do the same for a few more forms, then ask the Analyst something like: "Put these households together and count how many have no toilet, by village."

Try it on 3 or 4 real forms and tell me what it gets wrong, so I can tune it. After that I can add a way to read a whole folder of photos into one spreadsheet at once.
