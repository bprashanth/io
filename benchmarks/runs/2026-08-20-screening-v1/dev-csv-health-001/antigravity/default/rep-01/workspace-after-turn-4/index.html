1 How the 4-ANC Coverage Rate is Calculated

Standard Calculation Formula
ANC 4 Coverage Rate (%) =
ANC 4 Completed (anc4_completed) Pregnancies Registered (pregnancies_registered)
× 100

The rate measures the proportion of registered pregnant women who attended a minimum of 4 antenatal checkups out of all registered pregnant women in that district and calendar year.

Indicator Components:

  • Numerator (anc4_completed): Total pregnant women who successfully received 4 or more comprehensive antenatal care checkups during their pregnancy.
  • Denominator (pregnancies_registered): Total number of pregnant women registered for antenatal care services in district health facilities / community health centers within the year.
  • Multiplier (100): Standard scaling factor to express the resulting decimal proportion as a percentage (%).
