    How the rate is calculated

    The method behind every bar in this page.

    ANC4 coverage (%) = ANC4 completed ÷ Pregnancies registered × 100
    • ANC4 completed — pregnant women who completed 4 or more antenatal checkups (visits) during the year.
    • Pregnancies registered — all pregnancies registered at the facility in that district and year (the denominator).
    • The rate is computed per district per year from the counts above; the all-district total is the sum of completions divided by the sum of registrations (not an average of district rates).
