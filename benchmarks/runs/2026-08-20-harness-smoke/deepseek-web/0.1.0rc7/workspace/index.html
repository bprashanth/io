Formulas used

1. Training completion rate
Completion rate = (completed_training ÷ women_enrolled) × 100

Share of enrolled women who completed the training program.

2. Employment rate after 6 months (among completers)
Employment rate = (employed_at_6_months ÷ completed_training) × 100

Share of women who completed training and were employed when re-contacted 6 months after completion.

Year-over-year changes are shown in percentage points (pp) as Year B − Year A.
