Field Notes: A Week Between Kanhe and Khed Shivapur

I'm Arjun Patil, field coordinator with Sahayog Trust, and this week took me from Kanhe to Khed Shivapur for the quarterly livelihoods review. The state transport bus from Bhor was running more than an hour late, so I reached Kanhe only a little after eleven, by when Rukhsana Deshmukh had already gathered six other members of the self-help group under the neem tree outside the panchayat office. She was visibly annoyed about the delay but softened once I explained the bus had broken down near Nasrapur and every passenger had to wait for a replacement. Kanhe has had a good quarter -- the group's tailoring unit delivered forty school uniforms to the zilla parishad school last month and is now in talks for a second order from a nearby junior college. Rukhsana Deshmukh wants the Trust to co-sign a small loan so the group can buy a second-hand overlock machine, and I promised to take the request to the finance committee next week. Before I left Kanhe, she also asked me to note down that the bank has still not updated her passbook after the last disbursement, which is a separate problem for the accounts team to chase.

From Kanhe I walked over to the anganwadi, where Sunita Sayyed, the ASHA worker covering both Kanhe and the hamlets around it, was midway through an immunization drive. She had eleven children on her list for the day and only nine mothers had turned up, so she was calling the missing families one by one from a battered phone book. Sunita Sayyed mentioned that two of the mothers had shifted temporarily to Khed Shivapur for the sugarcane harvest and would need a follow-up visit there instead. She also flagged that the cold chain box lost power for an hour on Tuesday and she was not sure whether the vaccines already loaded were still usable; I told her to escalate that to the block medical officer directly rather than wait for our next visit.

The road on to Khed Shivapur is not motorable in the monsoon, so Sanjay Deshmukh, who has been driving for the Trust for six years now, took the longer route around the ridge. On the way he complained, not for the first time, that the field vehicle's insurance renewal is overdue and that nobody at the head office has confirmed the payment. Once in Khed Shivapur, the sarpanch, Ravi Kulkarni, was waiting with the water committee to discuss the borewell that failed again in April. Khed Shivapur has three functioning handpumps for close to four hundred households, and Ravi Kulkarni wants the Trust to fund a fourth, ideally before the next election cycle rather than after it, which he said plainly and without embarrassment. I told him honestly that this falls outside our current grant and that I would only be able to raise it as a proposal, not a promise.

Also in Khed Shivapur was Shabana More, one of the newer entrants to the tailoring batch, whose disability certificate application has been stuck at the taluka office for three months. The enrolment form the block office finally sent back listed her Aadhaar as 9909 9161 9504, which I have copied into the case file so we can quote it if the office asks again why the earlier submission was rejected. Shabana More is otherwise doing well in the batch and finished her first blouse piece unassisted this week, which the trainer flagged as a small but real milestone.

Back at the guesthouse in the evening I finally managed to reach Meena Shaikh, who handles community mobilization for the northern cluster and had been trying to loop me in on a joint visit to Kanhe next month. We agreed to align our calendars over email rather than phone, since the signal near Khed Shivapur keeps dropping; her address is meena.shaikh@sahayogtrust.in if anyone else needs to reach her before then, though the district office switchboard on +91 65454 57530 usually knows her whereabouts too. She also reminded me that the block coordinator, Vikram Iyer, wanted an update on the vehicle insurance issue Sanjay Deshmukh had raised, so I called him directly at +91 67198 74276 and left the same complaint with him that I had heard on the road that morning.

On a more irritating note, my own travel reimbursement of Rs 2,340 for last month is still stuck, apparently because the accounts team entered my account number, 66979697833805, IFSC HDFC0387116, incorrectly against an older passbook page. I have written to accounts@sahayogtrust.in twice now with a photograph of the correct cheque leaf attached and have not had a reply either time. Sanjay Deshmukh's own mobile, 8090828447, is on the same claim if anyone from finance wants to cross-check the odometer photographs he sent for the Kanhe-Khed Shivapur route.

Two more villages are on the list before month end, but for now this covers Kanhe and Khed Shivapur. I will circulate a shorter summary to the programme group by Friday, with the loan request from Rukhsana Deshmukh, the cold chain flag from Sunita Sayyed, and the water committee ask from Ravi Kulkarni as the three items that need a decision, not just an acknowledgement, from the head office.
