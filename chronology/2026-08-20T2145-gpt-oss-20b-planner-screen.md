# GPT-OSS 20B becomes the first sub-27B planner candidate

The smaller Qwen screen failed quickly: 8B returned no plan, 9B and 35B-A3B timed out, 14B timed out after one turn, and 30B-A3B lost the requested follow-up scope. We applied the declared early-stop rule rather than spending twenty questions on candidates already far behind the observed Antigravity behavior.

GPT-OSS 20B low passed the three-turn smoke in 30.694 seconds and $0.00027114. Its first ANC attempt exposed a change-versus-difference confusion. Medium effort repaired that issue but timed out at 120 seconds. A generic schema clarification and comparison-scope validator let low effort pass every critical ANC check in 57.782 seconds and $0.00079874; the retained noncritical miss is an unstated 6 pp 2023 gap whose two source values are visible.

On the safety-sensitive programme case, the model used the enrolled denominator for both rates, calculated 87/73 and 65/46, stated both 14/19 pp gaps, retained the two-row 2023 focus and produced the correct download. Failed repetitions showed that safety prose cannot be left entirely to the planner. The executor now requires a structured causal-limit flag and the trusted renderer always exposes the corresponding cause/intervention refusal. The counted run passed the full oracle in 41.424 seconds and $0.00064762.

Human screenshot review caught a renderer error after the automated tests passed: selecting Employment Rate updated the chart to 65/46, but the table still showed Completion Rate. The table now follows the selected indicator, the download still contains both fields, and the browser checker asserts that the selected indicator is present in the visible table header.

This is evidence for a constrained 20B planner, not arbitrary-file or product equivalence. The counted Antigravity references remain the comparison: 80.5 for the IDE smoke, 84 for ANC and 10 for the programme case. XLSX, PDF, web, irregular workbook structure, local execution and concurrency remain open.
